# Projects and updates

A **project** is a goal with a deadline that spans issues and teams,
such as "Policy simulator". It has a lead, members, teams, a start and
target date, milestones and a status.

## Health updates {#updates}

Progress is computed from the project's issues. **Health** is a human
call: on track, at risk or off track. The lead posts a short update each
week with the health and what changed; Vellum reminds them on the day
they pick.

::: stats

- 76% · Scope done · ▲ 9 pts
- At risk · Health · ▼ from on track
- 2027-01-10 · Target · ▬ unchanged

:::

**Initiatives** group projects and roll their health up, so leadership
sees one line per goal.

## Project pages {#pages}

Every project has a page. Embed the project's issues in it with a
collection fence, or put the PRD there and let its issue chips show
live status. The [PRD recipe](../cookbook/prd-with-issues.md) shows
both.
