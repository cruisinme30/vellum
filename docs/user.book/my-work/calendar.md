# Calendar feed and reminders

## Calendar feed {#ics}

Open **My Work → Calendars, capture and import** and choose **Get a new
feed URL** for a private ICS link. Subscribe to it in Google Calendar,
Apple Calendar or Outlook to see your dated tasks next to your meetings.
Treat the link like a password: it is shown once, and getting a new one
stops the old one working. **Turn the feed off** stops it for good.

Task apps that speak CalDAV, such as Apple Reminders and Thunderbird,
can also edit and tick off tasks. Copy the **CalDAV account URL**, sign in
with your handle, and use **Make an app password** for the password.

## Reminders and digest {#reminders}

Reminders arrive as web push, desktop and email notifications. They are
urgent, so they arrive during quiet hours too. The **daily digest**
arrives at the time you choose under **Time zone and reminders** in My
Work, with today's tasks, anything overdue and anything waiting on you,
and only on days that aren't clear. Both follow your own time zone.
Choose which channels each kind of notification uses in **Inbox →
Notification settings**.

## Capture {#capture}

::: facts

- Email: forward a message to your personal `tasks+…` address, from **Get a new
  address** in My Work's settings
- Slack: use the message menu's **Save as task**; the first time, Slack shows a
  link that connects your account
- Page: select text and press `⌘⌥↵`, or `T` on a page you can only read
- Comment: assign the comment to someone

:::

## Time-blocking {#time-blocking}

Drag a task from My Work onto your Google Calendar panel to block time
for it. The event links back to the task.

## Importing tasks {#import}

**My Work → Calendars, capture and import → Import** brings tasks in from
Google Tasks, Todoist or Things, keeping due dates, lists, subtasks and
recurrence where the source has them. Vellum counts the tasks in the file
before you import it, and importing the same file again adds only what
is new.
