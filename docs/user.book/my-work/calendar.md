# Calendar feed and reminders

## Calendar feed {#ics}

**My Work → ⋯ → Calendar feed** gives you a private ICS link. Subscribe
to it in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook to see your dated
tasks next to your meetings. Apps that support CalDAV can also tick
tasks off. Treat the link like a password; **Reset link** replaces it.

## Reminders and digest {#reminders}

Reminders arrive as web push, desktop and email notifications. The
**daily digest** arrives at the time you choose with today's tasks,
anything overdue and anything waiting on you.

## Capture {#capture}

::: facts

- Email: forward a message to your personal `tasks+…` address from **My Work → Capture**
- Slack: use the message menu's **Save to Vellum**
- Page: select text and press `T`
- Comment: assign the comment to someone

:::

## Time-blocking {#time-blocking}

Drag a task from My Work onto your Google Calendar panel to block time
for it. The event links back to the task.

## Importing tasks {#import}

**Settings → Import** brings tasks in once from Google Tasks, Todoist or
Things, keeping due dates, lists, subtasks and recurrence where the
source has them.
